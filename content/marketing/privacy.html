4. Health-related data

Information about the care you pay for can reveal details about your health, so we treat it as sensitive. We collect only what is needed to make and verify payments, and we process it with your explicit consent or where the law allows. We do not need your medical records or diagnoses to run your wallet.

When funders receive programme reports, we share aggregated or de-identified data wherever possible. We share identifiable data with a funder only where you have enrolled in their programme and agreed to it.

5. Mendy, our AI assistant

Mendy uses your questions and relevant account context to give answers. Mendy provides general information, not medical advice. We do not use your Mendy conversations to market third-party products, and we do not sell them. Avoid sharing details you do not want stored.

6. Who we share data with

  • Banking and payment partners: ProvidusUnity Bank, and Monnify as a backup processor, to hold funds and process payments.
  • Identity verification providers: to verify your NIN and BVN.
  • Healthcare providers: the provider you pay sees what they need to confirm and receive the payment.
  • Programme funders: aggregated reports, or identifiable data only with your agreement.
  • Service providers: cloud hosting, messaging and analytics suppliers bound by data processing agreements.
  • Regulators and authorities: when the law requires it.

We never sell your personal or health data.

7. International transfers

Some service providers may process data outside Nigeria. When this happens, we use safeguards required by the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023, such as adequacy decisions or contractual protections.

8. How long we keep data

We keep personal data only as long as needed for the purposes above. Financial and identity records are kept for the periods required by Nigerian financial regulations, typically at least five years after your account closes. Marketing preferences are kept until you withdraw consent.

9. How we protect your data

We use encryption in transit and at rest, strict access controls, two-person approval for fund movements, daily reconciliation, and regular security reviews. No system is perfectly secure, so we will notify you and the NDPC of a personal data breach where the law requires.

10. Your rights

Under the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023, you have the right to:

  • be informed about how we use your data
  • access a copy of your data
  • correct inaccurate data
  • ask us to delete data, where we are not required to keep it
  • restrict or object to some processing
  • receive your data in a portable format
  • withdraw consent at any time
  • complain to the Nigeria Data Protection Commission

To use any of these rights, email hello@menderplus.com. We respond within the time the law requires.

11. Children

Accounts are for adults aged 18 and over. Parents or guardians may manage care for their children through their own wallet. We do not knowingly create accounts for children.

12. Cookies and analytics

Our website and app use essential cookies and similar technologies to work, and analytics to understand how they are used. You can control non-essential cookies through your browser or device settings.

13. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. We will tell you about significant changes in the app or by email before they take effect.
