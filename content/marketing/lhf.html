Why LHF exists

Every health system runs on money, yet very few people understand how it moves. In Nigeria, about 72% of health spending still comes straight from households' own pockets, usually at the moment someone is already sick.

Better health systems need more people who understand how care gets paid for: not only economists and policymakers, but nurses, doctors, founders, students and funders too.

Mission

Help people understand how healthcare is financed, and equip them to build fairer ways to fund it.

Vision

A generation of Africans who understand health financing well enough to shape it.

Who it is for

Doctors, nurses, pharmacists, hospital managers, students, founders, policymakers, researchers, funders, and anyone curious about who pays for healthcare.

Our flagship event

The Naira Sessions: a quarterly event, every year.

Four times a year, LHF brings health professionals, policymakers, funders and innovators together to talk openly about healthcare financing: who pays, what works, and what has to change. Each session follows one big question, in plain language, with time for your questions.

What we champion

Financial protection

No one should be pushed into poverty to get treated.

Paying before illness

Savings, insurance and pooled funding, not cash at the worst moment.

Transparency

Health money should be traceable to the care it pays for.

Stronger domestic financing

Health systems funded and owned at home.

Health financing literacy

Plain-language knowledge for everyone, not just experts.

The next generation

Building the people who will finance Africa's health systems.
