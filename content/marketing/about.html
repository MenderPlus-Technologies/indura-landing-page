About us

Healthcare within reach.

Indura Health is a health access company. We build the rails, run the programmes and bring together the partners it takes to get people treated. Technology is how we deliver; healthcare within reach is why we exist.

Our story

Indura began with a family medical emergency, when the money failed at the point of care. Doctors were ready. The money was not. We are building a system where funds for care are protected before they are needed, and reach care when they are.
