LHF Note 1 · 3 min read

Who pays for healthcare?

Five sources pay for care: households, government, insurance, employers and donors. In Nigeria, households pay about 72%, mostly at the moment of illness. That is why one hospital visit can push a family into debt, and why countries that protect people pool money before illness, through taxes, insurance or savings.

Source: WHO Global Health Expenditure Database
LHF Note 2 · 3 min read

The world, by the numbers

The world spent about $9.8 trillion on health in 2022, yet 4.6 billion people lacked essential health services in 2023, and 2.1 billion faced financial hardship paying for care. More money alone does not mean more access; how it is pooled and spent matters.

Sources: WHO; WHO and World Bank, UHC Global Monitoring Report 2025
