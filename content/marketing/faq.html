General

What is Indura Health?

Indura is the financial operating system for healthcare in Africa. Families save for care, providers are paid at the point of care, and funders support treatment, with every naira traceable to the care it paid for.

Where does Indura operate?

Indura operates in Nigeria today.

Is Indura a bank?

No. Indura is a technology platform. Wallets, payments and settlement run through our banking partner, ProvidusUnity Bank, and Indura does not hold customer funds outside regulated institutions.

Wallet and payments

How does the health wallet work?

Download the app, verify your identity with your NIN, then save automatically or top up any time. Your balance can only be spent on healthcare at verified providers.

Can I withdraw my wallet balance as cash?

No. The wallet is locked to healthcare so the money is there when you need care. That is what protects it from everyday spending.

What is Fund Me?

Fund Me lets family and friends, including relatives abroad, add money directly to your health wallet.

Are there fees?

Saving and paying verified in-network providers is free. A small flat fee applies to payments to providers outside the network, and some international Fund Me transfers may carry a fee. Any fee is shown before you confirm.

What is Mendy?

Mendy is Indura's AI health and finance assistant. It helps you plan what to save, understand what care may cost, and find verified providers. It does not replace advice from a doctor or nurse.

Providers

How do I join as a provider?

Apply through our provider page. We verify your facility before it can receive payments. Joining is free.

When do providers get paid?

Wallet payments settle instantly at the point of care.

Institutions and programmes

How do funders use Indura?

Funders create ring-fenced programme wallets, enrol verified beneficiaries, and release funds only when care is delivered, with live reporting throughout.

How do I sponsor cataract surgeries?

Contact us through the contact page. Your funds sit in a ring-fenced wallet and are released to a vetted eye hospital only after a verified operation.

Data and security

What data do you collect?

Only what we need to verify you, run your wallet and pay providers safely. See our Privacy Policy for the full list.

Do you sell my data?

Never. We do not sell personal or health data.

Learn Health Financing

What is Learn Health Financing?

LHF is our community initiative helping people understand how healthcare is financed. It runs a weekly WhatsApp community and The Naira Sessions, a quarterly event.
