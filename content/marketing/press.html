About Indura Health

Short description

Indura Health is the financial operating system for healthcare in Africa. It helps families save for care, pays verified providers at the point of care, and lets funders track every naira to a verified patient.

Full description

Indura Health is a health access company based in Abuja, Nigeria, and a product of Mender Plus Technologies Ltd. Its platform connects families, healthcare providers and funders on one verified ledger: families save in a wallet locked to healthcare, providers are paid instantly when care is delivered, and institutions such as NGOs, employers and public programmes fund treatment with funds released only on verified care. Indura is licensed with the Nigeria Data Protection Commission, banks with ProvidusUnity Bank, and is available on Google Play and the App Store. Through Learn Health Financing, its community initiative, Indura hosts The Naira Sessions, a quarterly event on how healthcare is financed.

Founder bio

David Bobai, Co-founder and CEO

David Bobai is the co-founder and CEO of Indura Health, building the financial operating system for healthcare in Africa. He started Indura after a family medical emergency showed him how often care is delayed because the money is not there at the right moment. He also convenes Learn Health Financing, a community helping more people understand how healthcare gets funded. David completed the African Impact Challenge 2026 Bootcamp, supported by the University of Toronto and the Mastercard Foundation.
