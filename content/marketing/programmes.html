Launching now

Cataract surgery for adults.

Cataract is the leading cause of blindness in Nigerian adults, and it can be fixed with a short, routine operation. The barrier is rarely medicine. It is finding patients, paying for surgery, and reaching a hospital people can trust.

  1. Find: community screening identifies adults who need surgery
  2. Fund: sponsors pay into ring-fenced wallets
  3. Treat: eye hospitals are paid after a verified operation
  4. Follow up: outcomes are reported back to sponsors
