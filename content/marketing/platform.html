How a naira moves on Indura

Whether the money comes from a family, an employer or a donor, it follows the same verified path.

  1. Fund

    Money is added to a wallet or programme.

  2. Lock

    It can only be spent with verified providers.

  3. Verify

    The patient is confirmed by NIN before payment.

  4. Pay

    The provider is paid instantly at the point of care.

  5. Prove

    Every step is recorded for families and funders.

Payments to providers not yet on Indura go directly to a verified facility account, never to the user, and invite that facility to join the network.
